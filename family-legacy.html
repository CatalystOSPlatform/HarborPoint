Starting a Family Wealth Conversation

A legacy plan begins with more than documents.

Start with values

Discuss what money makes possible, what responsibilities come with it, and which causes or family goals matter most.

Choose the right participants

Not every discussion needs every family member at once. Start with the people most directly involved and expand as appropriate.

Make room for questions

Clear language and thoughtful pacing can support productive conversations, particularly when financial experience differs across generations.

Coordinate the details

Estate documents, beneficiary designations, and tax considerations require review with qualified legal and tax professionals.

Revisit as life changes

Families evolve, and a useful conversation is an ongoing practice rather than a single event.

For discussion, not as a recommendation.

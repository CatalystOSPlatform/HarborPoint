Market Uncertainty and a Long-Term Perspective

An investment plan is most useful when markets are uncomfortable.

Separate headlines from goals

Market news can be urgent without being personally actionable. Begin by reviewing your time horizon, income needs, and financial plan.

Understand risk in context

Risk tolerance and the capacity to take risk are not the same. Both matter when setting an asset allocation.

Use diversification deliberately

A diversified approach can spread exposure across different sources of risk, but cannot eliminate the possibility of loss.

Review, do not react

Periodic reviews can help identify when allocations or cash needs have drifted. A reaction to every headline can undermine a longer-term strategy.

For discussion, not as a recommendation.

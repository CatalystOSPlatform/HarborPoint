Five Questions to Ask Before Retirement

Retirement is more than a date on the calendar.

What would a satisfying retirement look like?

Start with the experiences, responsibilities, and priorities that matter most. Your spending plan should reflect more than a generic replacement-income target.

How reliable are your income sources?

Review Social Security timing, pensions, investment withdrawals, and other resources. Consider how each might change over a long retirement.

How might healthcare costs affect the plan?

Build room for premiums, out-of-pocket expenses, and possible long-term care needs. The appropriate assumptions depend on your circumstances.

How flexible is your plan?

Explore the effects of different market outcomes, inflation, family needs, and unplanned expenses. A useful plan includes room to adjust.

Who else should be involved?

Discuss tax-sensitive decisions with your tax professional and beneficiary or estate questions with qualified counsel.

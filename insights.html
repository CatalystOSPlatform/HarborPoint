Planning / Sample article

What Does Financial Independence Really Look Like?

Financial independence is personal. For some, it means retiring early. For others, it offers flexibility to pursue a second career or spend more time with family. Defining the destination is the first step in building a plan.

Start by separating essential spending from discretionary goals, then identify which resources need to support each priority. Revisit those assumptions as your life changes.

Business Owners / Sample article

Preparing for Life After a Business Sale

A liquidity event can transform your balance sheet and your daily routine. Planning well before a transaction can help you think through taxes, diversification, income, and what comes next.

It is equally important to consider personal purpose. A new financial chapter deserves a plan for how you want to spend your time, not only your capital.

Families / Sample article

Opening the Door to Family Wealth Conversations

Money can be a difficult family topic, especially when values, expectations, and generations meet. Begin with shared goals and the principles behind important decisions rather than specific account balances.

Regular conversations can help younger family members understand stewardship and build confidence in making their own choices.

Retirement / Sample article

Revisiting Retirement Through Life’s Transitions

Retirement planning is not a one-time calculation. Changes in health, family needs, inflation, market conditions, and personal interests all influence the picture.

Scenario planning can help identify which variables matter most and what choices may offer greater flexibility when the unexpected happens.

A Practical Perspective

Better questions lead to better decisions.

Before making a major financial move, ask what needs to be true for the decision to succeed, what risks you can control, and what would cause you to revisit the plan.

Clear decisions start with context.

Retirement planning

Preparing for Retirement: Five Questions Worth Asking

Retirement readiness is about more than a target account balance. Start with the spending that matters most, then identify potential income sources and when each may begin. Consider how much liquidity to keep available, whether your investment mix matches the timing of expected withdrawals, and what happens when markets decline early in retirement. Revisit healthcare costs and the possibility of a long retirement. Finally, decide how often the plan will be reviewed. An adaptable plan gives you a structured way to make decisions as circumstances change.

Business transitions

Before a Business Sale: Planning Beyond the Transaction

A sale can change income, taxes, lifestyle, identity and family expectations all at once. Owners may benefit from discussing their future cash needs, likely transaction structure, timing of major gifts and the risk of holding a large position in one asset. Collaborate with qualified legal, transaction and tax professionals early. The most useful planning question may be what the proceeds must make possible after the deal, not merely what the business might be worth.

Investment perspective

Market Volatility and the Value of a Long View

Short-term market movements can create a strong urge to act. A documented investment policy helps connect risk, liquidity and time horizon to a deliberate decision process. Diversification cannot eliminate losses, and no allocation is suitable for everyone. Reviewing how your portfolio fits within a broader financial plan may be more productive than trying to predict the next market move.

Legacy planning

Making Family Wealth Conversations More Productive

Conversations about legacy often begin with values, not documents. Families can discuss their goals for education, philanthropy, support and stewardship before focusing on specific structures. A qualified estate attorney can help translate those objectives into legal documents, while financial and tax professionals can coordinate implementation. Start with a shared purpose and make room for different perspectives.

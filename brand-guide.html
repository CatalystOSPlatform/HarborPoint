Fictional Brand Reference

A consistent voice across every conversation.

Public reference information for editorial and brand-gathering demonstrations. These conventions describe the imaginary Harbor Point Wealth brand and are not evidence of a registered advisory company.

Company description
A fictional Boston-based independent wealth management firm focused on personal guidance for families, professionals, and business owners navigating complex financial decisions.
Mission
Help clients make informed financial decisions that reflect their priorities and long-term goals.
Vision
Make financial planning feel understandable, connected, and human across life's changes.
Values
Clarity, stewardship, independence of thought, respect, and sustained partnership.
Positioning
Thoughtful, personal wealth management for people whose lives cannot be reduced to a portfolio.
Voice and tone
Measured, approachable, warm, precise, informed, and conversational. Favor specific explanations over superlatives or promotional promises.
Audience
Business owners, executives, pre-retirees, retirees, multigenerational families, and people navigating major life transitions.
Content pillars
Life transitions; retirement & income; investment perspective; family and legacy; business owner planning.
Words to use
Thoughtful, clarity, perspective, coordinated, priorities, planning, long-term, transition, partnership, informed.
Words to avoid
Guaranteed, risk-free, market-beating, effortless, always, never, exclusive returns, secret formula, promises of financial outcomes.
Writing conventions
US English, short informative headings, clear active voice, sentence-case body copy, specific language, no exaggerated claims.
Typography
Libre Baskerville or Georgia for editorial headings; DM Sans or Arial for body and navigation. Use accessible contrast and comfortable line spacing.
Colors
Deep navy #10283B; secondary navy #193C50; warm cream #F7F5EF; accent gold #BD9B69; body ink #243442.
Image direction
Boston harbor and New England coastal photography; authentic-feeling fictional portraits; natural light, restrained composition, calm, sophisticated atmosphere.
Logo use
Full-color lighthouse logo on light surfaces; white lighthouse logo on dark navy surfaces. Leave clear space and avoid distortion.
Approved claims
Only fictional company positioning and descriptive service language. No quantified outcomes, client counts, registrations, awards, financial performance, or legal qualifications are claimed.
Compliance rules
Never guarantee results, invent real credentials, suggest regulatory approval, or present fictional examples as actual client experience. Financial education is not personalized advice.
External platforms
No social accounts, CRM, or marketing analytics are connected to this fictional company. Links must be added and validated before any use.
Competitors
Comparable market category: independent wealth managers, registered investment advisers, boutique financial planning firms. No real commercial relationships or endorsements implied.
Partners
Potential collaborators in hypothetical client engagements include CPAs, estate attorneys, and business transaction counsel. None are named or represented as actual partners.

A thoughtful perspective on wealth, life, and the generations ahead.

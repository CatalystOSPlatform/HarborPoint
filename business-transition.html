Planning Beyond the Business Sale

A liquidity event can create new choices and new complexity.

Begin before the transaction

A thoughtful transition plan considers personal cash needs, ownership structures, and your intentions well before a sale is signed.

Coordinate professional expertise

Business attorneys, transaction specialists, tax professionals, and wealth planners have different but complementary responsibilities.

Define the next chapter

Consider the role of work, family, philanthropy, and lifestyle after leaving the company.

Plan for concentration and liquidity

A transaction can transform an illiquid business stake into liquid wealth. That shift may call for a review of portfolio construction, cash reserves, and risk.

Keep decisions connected

The closing date is a milestone, not the end of the planning process. Revisit choices as circumstances change.

For discussion, not as a recommendation.
